# Campaign mode — why Step 3.4 comes first and what it does

> Detail behind the summary in SKILL.md; only text SKILL.md does not carry. Normative.

Why Step 3.4 runs always and first (§5b): it runs first, because the other two gate on flags a campaign unit cannot otherwise have: the runner classifies once from the sub-iterate spec *text* and never reaches the Stage-2 Repo Scout, so every diff-driven detector is structurally silent until 3.4 re-decides from the real change set. It also raises the complexity F5c records — `check_integration_coverage` reads it there and green-SKIPs below `medium` — and STOPs the unit when the diff touches the CI trust boundary, since that acknowledgement is an operator's to write.
