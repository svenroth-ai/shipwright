Git calls inside the triage store's locked sections are bounded and report a timeout instead of raising: new `lib.git_base.run_git_soft` returns exit 124, and each site takes its own fail-safe direction. Previously `run_git` killed the process, stranding `.git/index.lock` — in `reconcile_triage` and `sweep_drift`, in the operator's own repository — and aborted `setup_iterate_worktree` after the worktree was created.
