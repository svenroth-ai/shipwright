The pull-request reviewer no longer spends its reading budget on a run's own review transcripts. `reviews.json` and the raw external-review replies are records *of* a review, so reviewing them is circular — and on a large change they were 19% of what the reviewer read, which was the difference between reviewing the change and refusing it as too big. The iterate spec and mini-plan stay reviewable: they state the acceptance criteria the diff should be read against.
