Triage findings are no longer stranded by a single unreadable character. A finding whose text carried a byte that is not valid UTF-8 was compared two different ways depending on whether it was read from the project's history or from the local buffer, so it never matched itself: the finding stayed in the local buffer forever and, in the worse case, the whole delivery step stopped and no findings reached the pull request at all. Both sides now read such a character the same way, and the exact original bytes are preserved.
