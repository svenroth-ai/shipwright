Recording a requirement as an unconfirmed assumption (`Basis: assumed`) now requires naming what would settle it — who to ask, or what to try — written as an acceptance criterion on that requirement rather than inside the cell, where a qualifier is a hard audit failure. The ban targets assuming in silence while someone is available to answer, not honest not-knowing. The `/shipwright-project` generation template no longer seeds requirement rows with that basis: a reader copying the template was previously put straight into violating the phase's own rule against it. The rule is now worded identically in the FR-authoring rulebook, the elicitation method, the generation template, the Basis vocabulary module and both iterate path references.
