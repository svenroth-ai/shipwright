`scripts/verify_local.py` — run CI's three bespoke merge guards (CI-gate guard, contract surface, sweep delivery surface) locally before pushing, in one pass that reports all three rather than stopping at the first failure. It drives each as a subprocess with the exact command `ci.yml` uses, prints whether it is vetting a clean or dirty working tree, and names the two CI-only gates it deliberately does not mirror. CI remains the authority.
