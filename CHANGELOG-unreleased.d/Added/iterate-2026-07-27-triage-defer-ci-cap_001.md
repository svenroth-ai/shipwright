`triage_cli.py defer <id> --reason <reason> --revisit YYYY-MM-DD` — the third decision on a triage finding (real work, deliberately not now) can be made from the terminal, not only from the Command Center. `triage_cli.py list` shows deferred entries in their own section with reason and revisit date instead of hiding them. The machine contract now exposes complete `open` and `deferred` sections under `contractVersion: 2` (a deliberate breaking change from the former open-only array).
